# House Guidelines

Welcome to Stays @ Studio Sun & Sea – a place for rest, practice, community, creativity, and reflection.

These guidelines are intended to help guests, residents, retreat participants, neighbors, and staff comfortably share the property and enjoy their time together.

Most of what follows can be summarized in four simple principles:
1. Be considerate.
2. Minimize harm.
3. Leave things at least as well as you found them.
4. Help create the experience you would hope to find when arriving as a guest yourself.

The spirit behind these guidelines is aligned with the yogic principle of *Ahimsa*.

*Ahimsa* is a Sanskrit word that is often translated as "non-harming" or "non-violence". It is a principle found throughout several dharmic traditions and yogic philosophies, and its application is simple and universal: when choices are available, seek the path that minimizes harm and supports wellbeing for yourself, others, and the broader community.

We do not expect perfection. We simply ask that everyone make a good-faith effort to approach the people and spaces around them with care, consideration, and respect.

The goal is not perfect compliance with some list of rules. The goal is to cultivate an environment where everyone can enjoy their stay in a way that supports both individual wellbeing and the wellbeing of the broader community.

## Quiet Evenings

Many guests come to Studio Sun & Sea seeking rest, reflection, creative work, recovery, or uninterrupted sleep.

To support this, we ask everyone to help maintain a peaceful atmosphere between 22:00 and 08:00. Conversations, music, and activities are welcome when they remain respectful of the people sharing the property.

## Gatherings & Community

Studio Sun & Sea exists to support connection, community, practice, and shared experiences.

Guests who would like to gather with friends or family are encouraged to make use of the rooftop terrace and other shared spaces designed for this purpose.

If you are planning something beyond a small informal gathering, please let us know. We are often able to suggest a suitable space, coordinate timing, and help ensure that everyone can comfortably enjoy the property.

## Children

Children are welcome at Studio Sun & Sea.

Many families appreciate the opportunity for children to explore, learn, play, and enjoy a degree of independence appropriate to their age and abilities. We believe that curiosity, responsibility, and freedom are valuable parts of growing up.

At the same time, parents and guardians remain responsible for helping children safely navigate the property, shared spaces, nearby roads, stairways, rooftops, wellness facilities, and the surrounding community.

We encourage families to find a balance between independence and safety that allows everyone to enjoy their time together.

## Animals

We understand that animals can be important companions and, in some situations, provide meaningful support.

At the same time, many guests choose Studio Sun & Sea because they are seeking a clean, low-allergen environment for rest, recovery, and wellness.

Because the impact of animals within shared hospitality spaces extends beyond a single guest, animals are generally not accommodated within guest units.

If an animal is an important part of your travel plans, we invite you to contact us before booking so that we can discuss whether an appropriate solution exists.

## Shared Spaces

Many of the property's spaces are intended to be enjoyed by everyone.

Shared facilities may include gardens, rooftop terraces, community areas, yoga spaces, and wellness amenities.

Please help care for these spaces by considering how your use may affect others, cleaning up after yourself, and leaving the space ready for the next person to enjoy.

## Wellness Amenities

Wellness amenities are intended to support rest, recovery, self-care, and enjoyment.

Please use all wellness facilities thoughtfully and with attention to your own health, comfort, and safety.

Every individual has different needs and limitations. We encourage guests to listen to their bodies and use wellness amenities in ways that feel supportive and sustainable.

## Property Care

Studio Sun & Sea has been created through significant time, effort, creativity, and care.

We ask guests to help steward the property by treating accommodations, furnishings, equipment, gardens, and shared spaces respectfully so that future guests may enjoy them as well.

## Safety

While we strive to create an environment that feels welcoming and secure, each person ultimately remains responsible for their own wellbeing and decisions.

If you notice a maintenance concern, potential hazard, or something that may affect the experience of others, please let us know so that it can be addressed promptly.

## Living Well Together

No document can anticipate every situation.

When questions arise, we encourage everyone to return to the principles found throughout Studio Sun & Sea:
- Be considerate.
- Minimize harm (*Ahimsa*).
- Leave things at least as well as you found them.
- Help create the experience you would hope to find when arriving yourself.

If you are ever unsure, please feel free to ask a member of staff or another member of the community. We are generally happy to help and would much rather answer a question than leave someone uncertain.
