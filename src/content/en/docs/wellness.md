# Practice & Wellness Guide

Welcome to the wellness spaces of Studio Sun & Sea.

This guide is intended to help guests, yoga and retreat participants, residents, and community members comfortably share the facilities that support practice, recovery, wellbeing, creativity, and reflection.

The spirit behind these recommendations is aligned with the principle of *Ahimsa* — seeking the path that minimizes harm and supports wellbeing for yourself, others, and the broader community.

The goal is not perfect adherence to procedures. The goal is to help everyone enjoy these spaces safely, respectfully, and sustainably.

## Practice & Wellbeing

Practice looks different for everyone.

For some, wellbeing is found through movement and physical activity. For others, it may be meditation, breathwork, journaling, creativity, bodywork, rest, conversation, or simply spending quiet time watching the sea.

We encourage each person to explore the practices that feel supportive, sustainable, and meaningful to them.

## Open Practice

When not reserved for scheduled classes, retreats, workshops, private sessions, maintenance, or other studio activities, the Sun & Sea Yoga Studio may be available for personal practice, meditation, journaling, breathwork, quiet reflection, or simply enjoying the space.

To ensure smooth transitions between activities, the studio may occasionally be unavailable before and after scheduled programming.

We ask everyone to help care for the space by returning props and equipment to their proper place and leaving the studio ready for the next person to enjoy.

## Shared Facilities

Studio Sun & Sea does not divide shared facilities, changing spaces, wellness amenities, bathrooms, or similar areas into separate categories.

We believe that all people can comfortably share spaces without first being sorted into predefined groups. Our focus is on respect, consideration, privacy, and individual responsibility rather than separation.

This approach extends to many categories commonly used elsewhere, including gender. While different communities and cultures organize shared spaces in different ways, we have found that most people are fully capable of navigating these environments respectfully when they are approached with openness, communication, and mutual consideration.

If you have questions or specific needs, please feel free to speak with a member of staff. We are generally happy to help find an approach that works for everyone involved.

## Caring for Shared Spaces

Many of our wellness amenities exist because they can be shared.

Yoga props, meditation cushions, towels, books, wellness facilities, and other resources remain available and enjoyable when each person contributes a small amount of care.

If you borrow equipment, please help ensure it is clean and returned to its proper place so that the next person can enjoy it equally well.

Used towels and linens do not need to be washed before being returned. Simply place them in the designated collection area so that they can be cleaned and prepared for future use.

The goal is not perfection. It is simply to leave shared spaces ready for the next person who arrives.

## Steam Spa & Recovery

Steam spas are intended to support relaxation, recovery, circulation, and wellbeing.

Every individual responds differently to heat, humidity, and recovery practices. We encourage people to proceed gradually, remain hydrated, take breaks when needed, and pay attention to how their body responds.

Longer sessions are not necessarily better sessions.

For many people, alternating periods of heat, cooling, hydration, rest, and reflection provides the most enjoyable experience.

## Sauna Culture

The rooftop sauna draws inspiration from Central and Northern European sauna traditions, where textile-free sauna use is common and widely expected.

People are encouraged to use a fresh towel as a barrier between their body and the wooden benches. This supports hygiene, comfort, and the shared enjoyment of the space.

Everyone arrives with different backgrounds, experiences, and comfort levels. Some may enjoy a textile-free sauna experience, while others may choose to wear something. We encourage each person to find the approach that feels most natural, comfortable, and supportive for them.

Many people encounter sauna traditions that differ from those they have previously experienced. We view this not as a challenge to overcome, but as an opportunity for curiosity, exploration, and learning.

Our goal is not uniformity. Our goal is to create a space where people can comfortably coexist, care for one another, and feel safe to explore and discover practices that support their own wellbeing.

## Swimming Pool

The swimming pool is intended to support relaxation, movement, recovery, and enjoyment.

Some people enjoy quiet reflection by the water. Others may use the pool for exercise, play, or connecting with friends and family. All of these experiences are welcome when approached with consideration for the people sharing the space.

As with other shared amenities, we encourage everyone to help care for the pool area by leaving it clean, returning borrowed items to their proper place, and helping create an environment that others can enjoy equally well.

People have different goals, abilities, comfort levels, and relationships with water. We encourage everyone to engage with the pool in ways that feel supportive, sustainable, safe, and appropriate for them.

## Outdoor Showers

Outdoor showers provide an opportunity to cool down, refresh, and reconnect with the natural environment.

They can be particularly enjoyable before or after time spent in a steam spa, following exercise, or after returning from the beach.

As with all shared amenities, we ask everyone to leave these spaces clean and ready for the next person to enjoy.

## Bodywork, Recovery & Rest

Many people arrive at Studio Sun & Sea carrying physical, mental, or emotional stress.

Wellbeing is not always found through doing more.

Sometimes the most supportive choice is to rest, slow down, spend time in nature, receive bodywork, enjoy a quiet conversation, or simply do nothing at all.

We encourage each person to approach wellness at their own pace and in a way that respects their personal circumstances and needs.

## Listening to Your Body

Every individual has different abilities, limitations, experiences, and goals.

Please honor your body's signals and make choices that feel appropriate for your current state of health and wellbeing.

If an activity, practice, or wellness modality feels unsuitable, uncomfortable, or unsustainable, it is perfectly acceptable to modify, pause, rest, or choose another approach.

## Shared Amenities

Certain amenities, such as the espresso machine, yoga props, books, bicycles, and other community resources, are intended to be enjoyed collectively.

If you use a shared resource, we simply ask that you help leave it ready for the next person to enjoy equally well.

## Community & Shared Experience

Many of the most meaningful experiences at Studio Sun & Sea emerge through spontaneous conversations, shared practice, meals, retreats, walks, workshops, and gatherings.

Community participation is always an invitation rather than an expectation.

Some people come seeking connection. Others come seeking solitude. Many alternate between the two.

We encourage everyone to respect both approaches and to help create an environment where each person can engage at the level that feels right for them.

The Café, patio, rooftop terrace, and other shared spaces are intended to support connection, conversation, learning, creativity, curiosity, and quiet reflection.

People are welcome to spend time reading, journaling, working, meeting new people, talking with friends, or simply watching the world go by.

We believe that meaningful community emerges when people are given permission to linger rather than feeling pressured to constantly consume, move on, or justify their presence.

## Exploring Brač

We encourage everyone to explore Supetar, the surrounding island, and Split-Dalmatia at their own pace.

A limited number of bicycles, e-bikes, and other mobility options for use around Brač are generally available for guests who wish to travel beyond walking distance.

When available, these resources are generally offered on a first-come, first-served basis.

For guests who would like the certainty of having a bicycle or other mobility option available at a specific time, advance reservations may also be available.

Our goal is to make exploring the island as accessible and enjoyable as possible while sharing these resources fairly among guests.

## Living Well Together

No guide can anticipate every situation.

When questions arise, we encourage everyone to return to the principles found throughout Studio Sun & Sea:
- Be considerate.
- Minimize harm (*Ahimsa*).
- Leave things at least as well as you found them.
- Help create the experience you would hope to find when arriving yourself.

If you are ever unsure, please feel free to ask a member of staff or another member of the community. We are generally happy to help and would much rather answer a question than leave someone uncertain.
