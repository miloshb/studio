## ✨ Core Voice Qualities

Studio Sun & Sea communicates with:

- **Warmth** — gentle, welcoming, human
- **Groundedness** — steady, calm, embodied
- **Clarity** — simple, clean, easy to understand
- **Mindfulness** — intentional, spacious, unhurried
- **Inclusivity** — accessible to all levels and backgrounds
- **Bilingual Ease** — English primary, German secondary, Sanskrit integrated respectfully

The voice should feel like a **deep breath** — spacious, warm, and steady.

## 🌿 Tone Principles

### **1. Warm & Inviting**
Tone should feel like a soft welcome, not a sales pitch.

**Use:**
- gentle invitations
- warm phrasing
- soft encouragement

**Avoid:**
- hype
- urgency
- pressure
- overly “spiritual influencer” language

### **2. Calm & Grounded**
The tone reflects the stillness of your homepage and the warmth of your color palette.

**Use:**
- slow, steady rhythm
- simple sentences
- natural imagery (breath, light, grounding)

**Avoid:**
- exclamation marks
- overly energetic language
- dramatic metaphors

### **3. Clear & Minimal**
Your design system is minimal — your language should be too.

**Use:**
- short paragraphs
- clean structure
- direct statements

**Avoid:**
- long, winding explanations
- jargon
- filler words

### **4. Inclusive & Accessible**
Everyone should feel welcome — beginners, advanced practitioners, all bodies, all backgrounds.

**Use:**
- “all levels welcome”
- “move at your own pace”
- “options and variations offered”

**Avoid:**
- elitist language
- assumptions about ability
- performative spiritual language

### **5. Multilingual with Ease**
- English is primary.
- German is integrated naturally.
- Sanskrit appears respectfully and with clarity.
- Croatian to be added once all content is stabilized and established across the business units.
- In the future, more languages may be added if there is sufficient participant desire.

Product names, retreat names, and program names remain in **English** across all languages. Descriptions, policies, schedules, and supporting content are translated.

**Format:**
**Sanskrit — English — German**
(using long dashes)

Example:
*Utkatāsana — Chair Pose — Der Stuhl*

## 🧘‍♂️ Writing Style Guidelines

### **Sentence Rhythm**
- gentle pacing
- natural pauses
- no rush
- no clutter

### **Vocabulary**
- warm, soft, grounded words
- avoid overly flowery or overly clinical language

### **Pronouns**
- Use **“you”** sparingly in teaching scripts
- Use **neutral voice** when cueing movement
- Use **“we”** when speaking about community

### **Punctuation**
- minimal
- no exclamation marks
- long dashes for Sanskrit–English–German

## 🪷 Examples

### **Website Hero**
**Breathe. Flow. Relax.**
Mindful movement for a calm, connected life.

### **Class Description**
A gentle, flowing practice that builds strength, steadiness, and presence.
All levels welcome. Options offered throughout.

### **Private Sessions**
A space for personalized guidance, mindful movement, and deeper connection.
Sessions available in English or German.

### **Teach With Us**
Studio Sun & Sea is a warm, collaborative space for teachers who value mindfulness, clarity, and community.
We welcome instructors who teach with presence and care.

## 🗣️ What to Avoid

- overly mystical or performative spiritual language
- high-energy marketing tone
- “transform your life” style claims
- excessive emojis
- aggressive CTAs (“Book now!”)
- personality-centric writing (“Milosh is the best teacher…”)

The brand is about **yoga, connection, and presence** — not about the individual teacher.
