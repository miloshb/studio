# Studio Sun & Sea — Color Palette
Warm–minimalist visual identity inspired by the Dalmatian coast, natural studio light, and the serene, calming atmosphere of mindful movement.

This palette is designed for:
- Website UI (Astro + Cloudflare)
- Mindbody widget overrides
- Marketing materials
- Social media templates
- Future collaborators and designers

---

## 🎨 Core Philosophy

Studio Sun & Sea uses **warm minimalism**:
- soft, serene tones
- gentle warmth
- natural light
- no harsh contrasts
- no neon or saturated colors
- a calm, poster‑like aesthetic

The palette is built around:
- **seashell pink warmth** (inspiration from "[Living Barre & Yoga](https://livingbarreandyoga.com.au/class/rooted-living/)")
- **Dalmatian coastal neutrals**
- **studio light tones** (from [Ellen Hoormann's space](https://ellenhoormann.de/yoga-lingen/))
- **subtle sea‑inspired accents**

## 🌅 Primary Palette

### **1. Seashell Warm — Primary Background**
<div style="width:200px;height:50px;background:#F7EFEA;border:1px solid #ccc;"></div>

A soft, warm, nearly‑neutral pink that creates the serene, calming base of the site.

- **HEX:** `#F7EFEA`
- **RGB:** `247, 239, 234`
- **Usage:**
  - main background
  - hero section
  - large whitespace areas
  - mobile backgrounds

### **2. Driftwood Sand — Primary Neutral**
<div style="width:200px;height:50px;background:#E8DCCF;border:1px solid #ccc;"></div>

A warm beige that grounds the palette.

- **HEX:** `#E8DCCF`
- **RGB:** `232, 220, 207`
- **Usage:**
  - section backgrounds
  - cards
  - subtle dividers

### **3. Deep Slate — Primary Text**
<div style="width:200px;height:50px;background:#3A3A3C;border:1px solid #ccc;"></div>

Soft charcoal for high readability without harshness.

- **HEX:** `#3A3A3C`
- **RGB:** `58, 58, 60`
- **Usage:**
  - body text
  - navigation
  - headings (when not using serif)

## 🧘‍♂️ Usage Guidelines

### **Backgrounds**
- Use **Seashell Warm** as the default background.
- Use **Sunlit Linen** or **Driftwood Sand** for section breaks.

### **Text**
- Use **Deep Slate** for primary text.
- Use **Soft Ink** for secondary text.

### **Buttons**
- Primary CTA: Deep Slate text on Seashell Warm or Terracotta Soft
- Secondary CTA: Terracotta Soft outline or Seafoam Mist fill

### **Borders & Lines**
- Use **Coastal Stone** for subtle dividers.
