- Minimum 3‑month commitment

- Classes do **not** roll over
- Not shown on website
- https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/classic/ws?studioid=5754454&stype=40&prodid=101

**Mindbody Description:**
> 5 classes per month with a minimum 3‑month commitment. Ideal for students practicing once per week. Unused classes do not roll over. Renews monthly until canceled after the minimum term.

**Agreement Terms:**
> Membership includes 5 classes per month.
> Minimum commitment: **3 consecutive months**.

>
> By purchasing, the client agrees to these terms.
